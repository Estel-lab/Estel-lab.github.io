## About Me

刘书宁 (Shuning Liu) is a PhD candidate in Microelectronics at HKUST(GZ), with a Master's degree from the Shanghai Institute of Technical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and a Bachelor's degree in Materials Physics from Shandong University. His research centers on infrared photodetectors—especially narrow-bandgap HgCdTe avalanche photodiodes (APDs)—mesoscopic carrier transport simulation, electric field engineering, and quantum-optics single-photon experiments.

He has published first/co-first-author papers in *IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices* (2026), *Optics Letters* (2025), *Infrared Physics & Technology* (2024), *Science China: Physics, Mechanics & Astronomy* (2023), and *Infrared and Laser Engineering* (2022), as well as a corresponding-author paper in *Infrared Physics & Technology* (2026). He has also co-authored papers in *Nature Photonics*, *IEEE Journal of Quantum Electronics*, *Optics Express*, and *Infrared Physics & Technology*, and holds a registered national software copyright for HgCdTe APD performance evaluation (`2023SR0426369`).
