Experimental construction, optical alignment, and coincidence characterization of a 405 nm pumped Type-II PPKTP polarization-entangled Sagnac interferometer photon-pair source.

- **Optical Architecture**: Bidirectional 405 nm pump in a polarization Sagnac loop featuring a dual-wavelength polarizing beam splitter (dPBS), dual-wavelength half-wave plate (dHWP), and temperature-tuned Type-II PPKTP crystal for quasi-phase-matched (QPM) degenerate photon-pair generation
- **Quantitative Alignment Workflow**: Two-iris beam collinearity calibration, mode-matching waist control, and PBS horizontal-polarization-referenced waveplate calibration
- **Photon-Counting Characterization**: Single-channel and coincidence-counting verification using a Swabian Time Tagger, progressing systematically from unidirectional pair verification to bidirectional balancing and polarization correlation measurements
